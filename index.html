RobRun Uploader

A small personal tool from RobRun Studios.

RobRun Studios creates short gameplay videos of RobRun. RobRun Uploader is a personal tool that posts those clips to the RobRun Studios TikTok account, so the same videos can be shared on TikTok and YouTube without uploading each one by hand.

How it works

  1. The account owner signs in with TikTok and approves the requested permissions.
  2. The owner chooses a gameplay clip and writes a caption.
  3. The tool uploads the clip to the owner's TikTok account using TikTok's official Content Posting API.

Who can use it

RobRun Uploader is used only by RobRun Studios to post its own original content. It is not a public service and does not post to anyone else's account.
