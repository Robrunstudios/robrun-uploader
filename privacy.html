Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how RobRun Uploader ("the App"), operated by RobRun Studios ("we", "us"), handles information when you sign in with TikTok and use the App.

1. Information we access

When you sign in with TikTok and approve access, the App may receive:

  • Basic TikTok profile information, such as your TikTok user ID (open ID), display name and profile picture, through the user.info.basic permission.
  • Access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok, which let the App act on your behalf within the permissions you approved.
  • Videos and captions you choose to upload. The App sends these to TikTok through the video.upload and/or video.publish permissions.

We do not ask for or receive your TikTok password.

2. How we use information

  • To sign you in and confirm which TikTok account to post to.
  • To upload the videos you select to your TikTok account.

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

3. Storage and security

Access tokens are stored only on the account owner's own computer and are used solely to talk to TikTok's official API. Videos are sent directly to TikTok and are not stored on any RobRun Studios server.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your information with third parties. The only service your information is sent to is TikTok, as needed to post your videos.

5. Cookies and analytics

This website does not use cookies, tracking or analytics.

6. Your choices

  • You can revoke the App's access at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions.
  • You can ask us to delete any information related to your account by emailing us. Locally stored tokens are deleted on request or when access is revoked.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

8. Changes

If we update this policy, we will post the new version on this page and change the effective date above.

9. Contact

Email robrunners10@gmail.com with any privacy questions or requests.
